# Operational Workflows

Asetio connects visibility, control, and execution. The main workflows revolve around assets, tags, gateways, alerts, and maintenance.

## Asset registration and traceability

The team registers assets with categories, custom fields, and metadata. Each asset can receive a QR code and a BLE tag to connect the digital record with the physical object.

## Tag discovery and assignment

Gateways detect nearby tags. From radar or the technician flow, the team can review discovered tags, register them, and assign them to existing assets.

## Coverage monitoring

Readings help teams understand which gateways are active, which zones have movement, and which assets were recently seen.

## Alerts and cases

Rules convert operational conditions into actionable cases. A case can include severity, history, assignment, notification channels, and deliveries.

## Maintenance

Plans and tasks coordinate preventive or corrective work. Technicians see assignments, search assets, link tags, and leave execution records.
