# Asetio Documentation

Asetio is an operations platform for organizations that need traceability over critical assets, real-time status, alerts, and field maintenance.

The documentation is organized around three layers:

- **Product:** what Asetio solves and who it is for.
- **Platform:** how tenants, users, assets, capture sources, and workflows are organized.
- **Status capture:** how field readings enter the system and become actionable state.
