# Introduction

Asetio is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for physical asset operations. Its goal is to show which assets exist, where they were last detected, which signals they reported, which risks are open, and what field work needs to happen next.

The product combines backoffice, inventory, asset identification, status capture, alerts, maintenance, a technician portal, and integrations.

## Problem

Many operations manage critical assets with spreadsheets, manual records, and systems that are disconnected from the field. That creates three problems:

- Inventory does not reflect what is actually happening.
- Field events do not always become action.
- Technicians lose context when executing maintenance or responding to incidents.

Asetio brings that operation into one platform: every asset has identity, history, operating location, updated status, related alerts, and maintenance tasks.

## Audience

- Operations teams that need real-time visibility.
- Maintenance teams coordinating field work.
- Infrastructure, facilities, warehouse, hardware, or critical equipment administrators.
- Companies that need tenant separation for organizations, users, permissions, and integrations.

## Main modules

- Asset inventory.
- Asset identification and assignment.
- Capture sources and zone coverage.
- Real-time radar.
- Alerts, cases, and escalation.
- Maintenance plans and tasks.
- Technician portal.
- API, webhooks, and integrations.
